About me

Fundamentals first. Projects always.

I'm a second-year Computer Science Engineering student who believes the best way to learn technology is to build with it. My journey started with C and the discipline of clean logic, grew through Python and SQL, and now spans everything from Flutter apps to Raspberry Pi hardware.

Classroom toppers tell you what a concept is; projects teach you what it's for. That's why my time goes into things like a cinematic Spider-Verse web experience, a children's learning app, a healthcare platform, and a smart attendance system — each one an excuse to connect theory with something real.

My goal is clear: become a full-stack, project-oriented developer who can move comfortably between fundamentals, web, databases, UI/UX, AI and IoT — and ship products that people genuinely use. Hackathons, team builds and technical presentations keep me sharp; curiosity keeps me going.
